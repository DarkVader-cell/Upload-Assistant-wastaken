# Aither slot recommendations — 2026-10-19

Based on the guidelines supplied by the operator. These are recommendations,
not a replacement for current site rules or staff decisions.

## Slot coverage

- SD: one WEB-DL, DVDRip and DVD Remux slot. Any known equivalent HD retail
  source precludes these; an empty site search does not prove no HD source
  exists elsewhere, so that prerequisite requires confirmation.
- 720p: one H.264 WEB and one H.264 encode.
- 1080p/1440p WEB: H.264, DV H.265, and HDR/DV-HDR H.265 slots.
  1440p requires confirmation that no 2160p source is available; a known matching
  2160p source blocks the recommendation.
- 1080p encodes: x264 Quality and Retention positions; under 20% difference
  competes for the same position. A sufficient size gap alone does not prove
  either release is transparent or a genuine quality/retention alternative.
  Separate x265 SDR, retail HDR/DV-HDR, and hybrid HDR10+/DV-HDR10+ slots.
- 1080p: one Remux slot, not one per codec.
- 2160p WEB: separate SDR, DV, HDR/DV-HDR and HDR10+/DV-HDR10+ slots.
- 2160p encodes/remuxes: separate SDR, retail HDR/DV-HDR and qualifying hybrid
  HDR10+/DV-HDR/DV-HDR10+ slots. HDR10+ does not itself prove a hybrid source.
- DVD5/DVD9/BD50/BD100 full discs have unlimited slots when contents or regions
  differ. Supported format and documented differences require verification;
  a differently named disc is not automatically different content.
- 3D is a separate slot dimension. Recognized actual cuts may coexist only
  with documented content differences. Distributor/remaster marketing labels
  do not manufacture slots. Open Matte/OAR extra slots need prior staff approval.

DV/HDR takes precedence over HDR in its slot; DV/HDR10+ takes precedence over
HDR10+ (and DV/HDR in the hybrid slot), subject to the applicable source rules.
Dropping HDR10+ is not silently treated as an upgrade. Retail disc DV normally
takes precedence over hybrid sources, but the proven-superior hybrid exception
requires review. Every hybrid upload needs grade checks for each injected
source and an HDR10+/L1 plot where applicable; flags and filesize cannot prove
those requirements.

## Evidence and exceptions

WEBRips require **10 comparison screenshots** and verified higher-resolution
WEB-DL provenance, superiority, or absence of a WEB-DL. WEB-DL takes precedence.
1080p downscales from 2160p WEB need superiority to Blu-ray and Encode category;
they compete for x264 encode slots. Animation exceptions need comparisons and
compete for the respective encoding slots. Relabelling a declared WEB-derived
encode does not erase these requirements. UA does not fabricate source height
or change the upload category automatically.

Aither internals may coexist in an additional slot or replace an equivalent/
superior release. Internal status alone never establishes quality. Upload
internal eligibility is taken from the enabled Aither internal setting and a
matching configured internal group, not an INTERNAL word in the filename.
Explicit ASL/BSL/BASL/AD additional-slot claims require internal eligibility and
verification of the actual features. Disc extras and other extra-slot requests
require justification/review. UA does not infer accessibility from an `AD` word
in a movie title or invent staff approval.

Required source/comparison/approval evidence is marked separately from a basic
duplicate. Unattended runs do not silently accept such REVIEW decisions,
including vacant slots. Attended confirmation defaults to No. A bandwidth wait
that discovers new evidence requirements skips submission for a fresh review.
A standalone/coexisting slot does not offer unrelated releases as trump-report
targets. Only actual competitors/replacement candidates enter that choice.

Metadata declarations, native screenshots, transparency, actual content changes,
source lineage and staff approval still need human verification. The engine
cannot prove all of them from a release name, API flag, or percentage alone.
